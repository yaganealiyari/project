Down And Out

Kenny Hansen
May 15, 2023
1 Comment

He sat across from her trying to imagine it was the first time. It wasn't. Had it been a hundred? It quite possibly could have been. Two hundred? Probably not. His mind wandered until he caught himself and again tried to imagine it was the first time.

She noticed the warmth in his voice and the way he looked at her when he thought she wasn't paying attention. There was something comforting about his presence, something that made her feel at home even in this unfamiliar café.
